## Abstract

Product Entity Resolution (PER) in e-commerce is challenging due to unstructured text, formatting inconsistencies, promotional noise, and fine-grained product differences. We present an end-to-end evaluation of four zero-shot PER pipelines---Lexical Cascade, Semantic Cascade, Hybrid Fusion Cascade, and Generative-Lexical Cascade---across established benchmarks and introduce two real-world datasets characterized by cross-lingual noise and dense brand collisions. We evaluate lexical and dense blocking with cross-encoder, tensor-search, and Large Language Model verification, considering accuracy, runtime, and memory consumption.
